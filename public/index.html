05 / Questions

The practical details.

How does matching actually work?

Each candidate is scored out of 100 across five weighted components: skill overlap (40), availability against your start window (20), budget fit (15), reputation from completed projects and reviews (15), and settlement compatibility (10). The shortlist shows the breakdown and the plain-language reasons, so you can disagree with it.

What does escrow protect?

Clients fund a milestone before work starts, which moves the amount plus the 3% platform fee out of their available balance. Talent can see the funds are committed. Release requires the client to approve delivered work; a refund returns everything to the client and resets the milestone.

Are the crypto payments real?

No. This build simulates balances, escrow, and settlement inside the application database. No wallet is connected and nothing is broadcast to any chain. The data model is shaped so a real payment provider could be substituted behind the same API.

Who reviews the talent?

New specialist profiles start as pending and are invisible in the directory and in matching until an operator approves them in the control room. Operators can also mark a profile verified, which adds a small reputation bonus.

What does it cost?

A 3% platform fee is added on top of each milestone when the client funds it. Talent receive the full milestone amount on release.
